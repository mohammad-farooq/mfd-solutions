Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start working together.

What services do you offer? +
I offer a full range of IT services including web development, mobile app development, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, AI & automation, UI/UX design, and ongoing technical support.
How long does a typical project take? +
Project timelines vary based on complexity. A simple website takes 2-4 weeks, while complex web applications or mobile apps can take 2-4 months. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.
Do you provide ongoing support? +
Absolutely! I offer 24/7 support and maintenance packages. Every project includes free support for 1-6 months depending on the package you choose.
What is your payment structure? +
Typically 40% upfront to start the project, 40% at the midpoint, and 20% upon completion. For larger projects, I can offer flexible payment plans.
Can you work with my existing website or app? +
Yes! I can audit, redesign, optimize, or extend your existing website or application. I'll review your current setup and provide recommendations.
Do you sign NDAs and protect my intellectual property? +
Absolutely. I sign NDAs before discussing project details and ensure all intellectual property rights are fully transferred to you upon completion.
